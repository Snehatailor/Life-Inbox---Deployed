How it works
  • Finish tasks to earn coins: High 30, Medium 20, Low 10.
  • Done a day or more early: +5. Overdue: half coins.
  • Reach 100 coins in a day to keep your 🔥 streak.
  • Binned tasks keep their coins. Reopening a task takes them back.
  • Say or type a task, drag tasks onto a priority or a day, and drop files anywhere.
